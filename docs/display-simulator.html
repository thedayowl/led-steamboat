How it's built

      
Each frame member gets length ÷ spacing bulbs, rounded, spread evenly along its traced shape. With measured lengths the count uses the length written on the sketches; with photo lengths it uses the length of the traced shape. They differ most on the clouds. Smoke puffs 1 and 2 use their photo lengths either way, because the lengths written on the sketch were far longer than the wire.

      
The smoke puffs are the three clouds: puff 1 right above the stack crown, puff 2 the big middle cloud, and puff 3 the cloud that spans the seam between panels 2 and 3, whose two halves light together.

      
The water treats every waterline bulb as one strip in bow-to-stern order, the way the section's runs will be listed, so the waves move toward the stern, as if the boat were moving forward. The wheel's sets are A, B, C, A, … round the 16 spokes, ordered so the top of the wheel moves toward the bow, the way a stern wheel pushes the boat forward; wheeldir flips it.

    

  

  
Bulbs are drawn front-on at C7 size (7/8″ across) to the same scale as the frame, so zooming in shows the real gaps between them. Frame shapes and lengths come from the layout traced from the photos; the waves at the bottom of panel 3 and the tip of the bow are placed from the sketches. Timing, levels and the water, smoke (FFB060) and spoke (FF0030) colours come from config.h and display.cpp. The screen version is gamma-corrected to look like LED light; real bulbs are far brighter.
